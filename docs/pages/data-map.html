Ground rules for both conversations

Decided in the M2 compliance bar and the ADRs. These apply to every dataset below.

Roles

The Sponsor is controller, we are processor. A DPA and a subprocessor list with regions are signed before real data arrives.

Read-only sources

We never write back into the EDC, CTMS or any other Sponsor system. Only data born on our platform is ours to change.

Pseudonymized Subjects only

EDC subject ID, Site, status and status dates, screen-fail reason category, next visit date. Every other field is dropped while parsing, before anything is written (ADR 0001).

Residency

EU by default where it costs nothing extra: Supabase EU, Vercel fra1, an EU email provider. Not a go-live gate. Anthropic is a disclosed subprocessor under SCCs with zero data retention.

Audit trail

Append-only, timestamped, attributable, before/after values. Covers sign-ins, approvals, sends, AI drafts and classifications, syncs, settings, exports, and every read of Subject data by a user or by Prime.

Part 11-ready, not validated

Approvals record approver, time and signature meaning. No re-authentication at approval, no validation package, no CSV/CSA.

Retention

Rolling periods set by the Sponsor per data class at onboarding, no defaults. Nothing is purged until a period is set. At contract end: export to the Sponsor, then delete.

Security

Encryption in transit and at rest. Email magic-link sign-in, SSO optional. Review pack: CAIQ-lite / SIG Lite, data-flow note, DPA, subprocessors. SOC 2, ISO 27001 and pen test deferred.

Never ingested

In either conversation. Fields outside the allowlist are dropped while parsing, before anything is written.
