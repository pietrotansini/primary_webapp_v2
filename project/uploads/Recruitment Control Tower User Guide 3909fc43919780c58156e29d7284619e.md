# Recruitment Control Tower | User Guide

When only **20% of clinical trials complete recruitment on time[1],** biotech teams need a real-time way to identify underperforming sites, understand why enrollment is slipping, and act before delays become irreversible.

The Recruitment Control Tower turns fragmented site data into early warnings and corrective actions.

## Turn recruitment data into faster enrollment per site

Instead of collecting reports from multiple systems or waiting for CRO updates, the platform continuously brings together recruitment data from your operational systems and translates it into clear insights and recommended actions.

---

# How to use the platform

### 1. Start with the Overview

When you log in, you see the health of your study recruitment.

The dashboard highlights:

- Current enrollment versus target
- Forecasted enrollment completion
- Number of active, recruiting, and stalled sites
- Recruitment funnel performance
- Sites requiring immediate attention
- Overall recruitment risks

Within a few seconds, you should understand whether your study is on track and where your attention is needed.

---

### 2. Review your highest-priority sites

The platform automatically ranks sites according to recruitment risk and expected impact.

For every site you can immediately see:

- Current enrollment progress
- Expected recruitment pace
- Funnel bottlenecks
- Recent changes
- AI-generated explanation of the likely causes
- Recommended next steps

Rather than searching for problems, the platform brings the most important ones to you.

---

### 3. Understand why recruitment is slowing

Selecting a site opens a detailed operational view.

Here you can explore:

- Patient recruitment funnel
- Referral and screening performance
- Screen failure patterns
- Enrollment trends
- Site activation status
- Recent operational events
- Recruitment history

The AI summarizes all available information into a clear explanation of what is happening at the site and why.

---

### 4. Take action

The Actions page is your operational workspace.

The platform continuously recommends the interventions most likely to improve recruitment, such as:

- Contact a site coordinator
- Escalate an issue to the CRO
- Review screening criteria
- Improve referral quality
- Launch or adjust recruitment campaigns
- Support patient logistics
- Reallocate recruitment targets

Each recommendation explains:

- Why it was generated
- The supporting evidence
- Expected impact on enrollment
- Suggested owner
- Priority
- Confidence level

Where possible, the platform also prepares draft communications that can be reviewed and sent with minimal effort.

---

### 5. Track results

As new recruitment data arrives, forecasts and recommendations update automatically.

This allows your team to quickly verify whether actions are improving recruitment and focus attention where it is needed most.

---

# What data is used

The Recruitment Control Tower brings together operational data from multiple systems, including:

- IRT / RTSM
- Electronic Data Capture (EDC)
- Clinical Trial Management Systems (CTMS)
- Site activation and startup platforms
- Recruitment and referral management systems
- Site systems (eSource, eReg, eISF)
- Patient engagement, logistics, and retention platforms
- CRO operational reports

By connecting these previously disconnected sources, the platform creates a single, consistent view of recruitment performance.

Biotechs should not depend on CROs to keep trials recruitment on track.

The Recruitment Control Tower continuously monitors recruitment activity, highlights what has changed, explains the underlying causes, and recommends the next best actions, helping your team keep studies on schedule and bring therapies to patients sooner.
