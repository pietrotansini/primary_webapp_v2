Turn 1 · Swiss cross × #
Three ways to merge the cross and the hash
1a Forked cross
A # with its centre filled in. The middle becomes the Swiss cross, and the ends split into two prongs. Both readings hold up, even at 20px.
1b Hash at the heart
Stays closest to the flag. A small red # is cut into the centre of the cross. It's best at 48px and up; at icon size it reads as just the flag.
1c Leaning hash
Same build as 1a, but the verticals slant the way a typed # does. It reads more clearly as a hashtag and has a sense of forward motion.
